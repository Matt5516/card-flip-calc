CGC Cards grading fees (2026)

Every CGC Cards service tier with its price, value cap and turnaround, plus what a graded card has to sell for just to pay back the fee.

CGC Cards grading starts at $17.00 per card (Bulk, 25-card min (150 working days)) as of October 14, 2026. A card graded at that tier must sell for about $37.35 on eBay just to cover the fee, shipping and selling fees, before counting what you paid for the card.

How to pick a CGC tier

Choose the cheapest tier whose maximum declared value is at or above what the card will be worth after grading. If a card comes back worth more than the tier allows, graders charge an upcharge to the right tier, so a card you expect to be a high-value 10 should be declared at that value up front.

The calculator does this automatically: set the company to CGC, leave the service tier on “Auto”, and it picks the cheapest tier that covers your highest graded value.

Worked example

A card you could sell raw for $40 that sells for $150 as a 10. At Bulk, 25-card min (150 working days) ($17.00), total cost is the $40 card plus $17.00 plus $10 shipping = $67.00. A 10 nets about $124.72 after eBay fees and shipping, so the profit at a 10 is $57.72. Anything below a 10 usually loses money on a card like this.

FAQ

How much does CGC grading cost in 2026?

CGC Cards starts at $17.00 per card (Bulk, 25-card min (150 working days)) as of October 14, 2026. Higher-value and faster tiers cost up to $300.00.

What is the fastest CGC service?

WalkThrough (2 working days) at $300.00 per card.

Does the CGC fee include shipping?

No. Shipping to the grader and return shipping with insurance are extra. Many submitters split one shipment across several cards to cut the per-card cost.
