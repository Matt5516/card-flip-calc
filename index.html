How the math works

Total cost = card cost + grading fee + shipping to grader + return shipping + other costs.

Net profit at a grade = sale price − eBay fees − your shipping to the buyer − total cost.

eBay fee = final value fee % × (sale price + sales tax), up to $7,500, plus 2.35% above $7,500, plus $0.30 per order ($0.40 over $10).

Break-even = the lowest sale price where the net after eBay fees and shipping covers your total cost.

Recommendation compares the probability-weighted graded profit to the profit from selling raw today.
