Is it worth grading Yu-Gi-Oh! cards?

In this example, a $60 raw card needs about $109.97 graded to break even at CGC ($20.00). The odds-weighted profit is -$22.27 vs -$13.35 selling raw, so the call is “sell raw”. Swap in your own odds and prices.

Worked example

The card costs and sells raw for $60. Assumed graded sale prices are multiples of raw: 10 = 3.5×, 9 = 1.4×, 8 = 0.9×, 7 or lower = 0.6×, with grade odds of 15% / 35% / 30% / 20%. These are illustrative, not market data. Use sold comps and your own read of the card.

What differs for Yu-Gi-Oh! cards

Yu-Gi-Oh! cards from early sets often have off-center prints and soft corners, so true 10s can be scarce, and a 10 can sell for many times a 9 on key cards. Modern foils may show surface scratches. Confirm your chosen grader accepts them and check the pop report for your set.

Grader acceptance and category rules change, so confirm on the grader’s own site (PSA · SGC · BGS · CGC). Fees as of October 14, 2026.
