# GradeOrFlip

Free card grading calculator: compare PSA, SGC, BGS and CGC fees, shipping and eBay fees to see profit, break-even and the odds of a 10 you need to submit.

**Live site: [https://gradeorflip.com/](https://gradeorflip.com/)** (free, no signup, runs in your browser)

## Tools
- [Grading calculator](https://gradeorflip.com/grading-calculator/): Should you grade this card? Profit, break-even and odds for PSA, SGC, BGS and CGC.
- [Bulk submission planner](https://gradeorflip.com/bulk-planner/): List several cards and see which ones are worth sending in one order.
- [eBay fee calculator](https://gradeorflip.com/ebay-fee-calculator/): What you take home after eBay fees, shipping and sales tax.
- [Turnaround tracker](https://gradeorflip.com/turnaround-tracker/): Track your submissions and see when each order should come back.
- [Submission checklist](https://gradeorflip.com/submission-checklist/): A printable checklist so nothing goes wrong when you ship cards.

## Data sources
Grading fees, turnaround times and tiers come from the public pricing pages of PSA, SGC, Beckett (BGS) and CGC, and eBay fee figures come from eBay's published fee schedule. Each figure on the fee pages shows its source and the date we last checked it. Fees change often, so confirm on the grader's own site before you submit. Your card values, grade odds and resale prices are your own inputs; we do not look up card prices.

## About this repo
Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript on GitHub Pages: no backend, no ads, no cookies (cookieless GoatCounter visit counts only). The math lives in `assets/calc.js` and is covered by automated tests. Pages are generated by a shared build script, so the HTML here should not be edited by hand. Feedback and corrections: [open an issue](https://github.com/Matt5516/card-flip-calc/issues).

Estimates only, not financial advice. Grades are never guaranteed. Not affiliated with PSA, SGC, Beckett, CGC or eBay.
