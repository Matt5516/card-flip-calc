eBay fees for trading cards (2026)

eBay charges 13.25% of the total sale (including shipping and sales tax) up to $7,500, 2.35% on the part above that, plus $0.40 per order ($0.30 if the order is $10 or less) for trading cards, as of October 14, 2026.
